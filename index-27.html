The physician

Trained at the top,
practicing where
you need him

Dr. Porto (Dennis A. Porto) graduated at the top of his class from the University of Iowa College of Medicine, earning membership in the Alpha Omega Alpha honor society. He completed a skin cancer research fellowship and later a Master of Public Health at Harvard, where he was awarded the Lewis Hackett Scholarship.

After completing internship and a three-year dermatology residency, Dr. Porto pursued advanced subspecialty training in Mohs micrographic surgery — the most precise technique for skin cancer removal — and is double board-certified in both dermatology and Mohs surgery.

He is co-author and editor of the definitive textbook on Botox and cosmetic injectables, author of dozens of peer-reviewed articles, and a recipient of the Golden Scalpel from the American Society for Dermatologic Surgery. He holds a faculty appointment at Mount Sinai, where he teaches skin cancer surgery.

Common questions

Frequently asked

What is virtual dermatology?

Virtual dermatology (also called teledermatology) lets you consult with a board-certified dermatologist from your phone or computer. You can send photos, describe your symptoms, receive a diagnosis, and get a prescription — all without an in-person visit.

What conditions can Dr. Porto treat virtually?

Dr. Porto treats acne, rosacea, eczema, psoriasis, hair loss, melasma, hyperpigmentation, seborrheic dermatitis, perioral dermatitis, keratosis pilaris, warts, cold sores, fungal infections, nail conditions, hives, contact dermatitis, skin tags, spot checks for moles, rashes, itching, and scars — all through virtual consultation.

Is Dr. Porto board-certified?

Yes. Dr. Porto is double board-certified in both dermatology and Mohs micrographic surgery. He is Harvard-educated (MPH) and holds a faculty appointment at Mount Sinai.

Which states does Dr. Porto practice in?

Dr. Porto is licensed to practice in New York, Massachusetts, California, Virginia, Maryland, New Jersey, New Hampshire, Hawaii, Pennsylvania, and Connecticut, with more states coming soon.

Can I get a prescription through a virtual dermatology consult?

Yes. When medically appropriate, Dr. Porto can prescribe medications for skin conditions during a virtual consultation, and prescriptions are sent directly to your pharmacy.
